Guest Contribution & Pitch Guidelines

We welcome cybersecurity practitioners, security analysts, network engineers, and tech writers to share actionable knowledge with our audience.

Content Criteria

  • Originality: All submissions must be 100% original, unique, and unpublished elsewhere.
  • Actionable Focus: We prefer hands-on tutorials, architecture diagrams, step-by-step hardened configs, or real-world incident case studies.
  • Article Length: Long-form content (1,200 to 2,500 words) with clear code snippets or CLI examples.
  • Acceptable Topics: Endpoint Detection, Cloud Security (AWS/Azure/Cloudflare), Threat Hunting, SIEM/SOC Operations, and Secure Coding.

Submission Process

  • Send your topic outline and 2–3 previously published writing samples.
  • Include your target keywords and proposed target audience expertise level.
  • Once approved, submit drafts in clean Markdown or Google Docs format.
